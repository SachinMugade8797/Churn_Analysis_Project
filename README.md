# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis & Customer Intelligence

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs an end-to-end **Customer Churn Analysis** to understand customer behavior, subscription patterns, churn drivers, and customer support interactions.

The project combines data from multiple sources stored in a **SQLite database**, cleans and transforms the data using Python, performs feature engineering and exploratory data analysis, and generates business-focused insights using visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Customer churn directly affects recurring revenue and customer lifetime value. The objective of this analysis is to answer key business questions and provide actionable insights.

## 📊 Key Insights Summary

- **Churn Rate:** 28.6% | **Retention Rate:** 71.4%
- **Highest Churn Plan:** Basic (no major revenue impact)
- **Peak Churn Period:** September 2024
- **Most Affected State:** Karnataka
- **Average Tenure:** 1,451 days
- **ARPU:** ₹18.8 | **Total Revenue:** ₹395
- **Revenue Loss:** ₹74 (18%)
- **CLTV Lost:** ₹2,047
- **Monthly vs Annual Churn:** 55.6% vs 8.3%

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- ✅ Focus on **monthly customers** – significantly higher churn rate
- ✅ Investigate high churn in **Karnataka**
- ✅ Analyze churn surge in **September 2024**
- ✅ Monitor high-risk customers with proactive retention offers
- ✅ Prioritize retention on high **CLTV** customers
